## 1.0 SCOPE

This specification covers performance, tests and quality requirements for the USB Type C Receptacle USB4105 series.

- 2.0 PRODUCT NAME AND PART NUMBER

USB Type C Receptacle USB4105 series.

- 3.0 PRODUCT SHAPE, DIMENSIONS AND MATERIAL

Please refer to drawing.

## 4.0 RATINGS

- 4.1 Current rating: 5.00A collectively for VBUS pins(i.e., pins A4, A9, B4, and B9)

   - 6.25A collectively for GND pins(i.e., pins A1, A12, B1, and B12)

   - 1.25A for VCONN (i.e., A5/B5)

   - 0.25A for all other pins, per pin

- 4.2 Voltage rating ……………………….  48V DC

4.3 Power rating …………………………  240W

4.4 Operating Temperature Range ……  -40°C to +85°C

## 5.0 TEST AND MEASUREMENT CONDITIONS

Product is designed to meet electrical, mechanical and environmental performance requirements specified below. All tests are performed in ambient conditions unless otherwise specified.
